About

Duane Johnson, ChFC®  CLU®

Founder & Principal

Quarters Wealth Management, LLC

ChFC®CLU®B.S. Math & Economics

Duane Johnson is the Founder and Principal of Quarters Wealth Management, LLC. He brings more than two decades of experience in financial services and holds both the Chartered Financial Consultant (ChFC®) and Chartered Life Underwriter (CLU®) designations. He earned a Bachelor of Science with a double major in Mathematics and Economics from Eastern Michigan University.

Duane's approach is grounded in a simple conviction: the plan comes first. Every investment decision, every portfolio adjustment, every client conversation flows from a financial plan built around that client's life, goals, and true tolerance for risk.

Outside the office, Duane supports Bendix Academy, Brighton High School Marching Band, The Arc, and Special Olympics. Duane lives in Howell, MI where he practices the hobby of beekeeping. He enjoys collecting and giving the honey to clients, family and friends.

“I owe them (clients) everything we expressly agreed to and, to an extent, what they think we agreed to.”- Duane Johnson, ChFC® CLU®
