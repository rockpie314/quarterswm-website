How We Invest

Eight principles that guide every portfolio decision.

Rather than attempting to forecast markets, this approach emphasizes durability, alignment, and disciplined decision-making across full market cycles.

01

Goals Come First

Portfolios are designed around purpose, time horizon, and real-world risk tolerance.

02

Risk Management Is Fundamental

Protecting capital is essential to long-term compounding.

03

Intentional Diversification

Assets are selected for distinct economic and market behavior.

04

Asset Allocation Drives Results

Allocation defines portfolio behavior; security selection refines outcomes.

05

Quality and Valuation Matter

Emphasis on durable cash flows and strong balance sheets.

06

Discipline Over Emotion

A structured process reduces reactionary decision-making.

07

Rebalancing Maintains Alignment

Risk exposure is actively managed as markets evolve.

08

Cost and Tax Efficiency

Fees, taxes, and turnover are minimized wherever possible.
