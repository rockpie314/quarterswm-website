A map and a message

This document is not a will, a trust, or any other legal or financial instrument. It is simply a gift — a map and a message, written with intention, so that the people who matter most to me do not have to guess.

For couples: the personal sections (About Me, Final Wishes, A Personal Note) belong to each of you individually — fill out your own copy. The factual sections (finances, legal, property, family) can be shared in a single copy between you.
